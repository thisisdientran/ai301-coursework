# Voice guide: how I talk upstream
## Who I am in threads
Hi, I am a newbie to open-source contributing. I have expertise in C++, Javascript, Java, and Python. I confident with working on the front end and still learning on working on the back-end

## Rules I write by
### Rule: Must have the version
- Wrong: "Hi, I work on this version"
- Right: "I worked on version 1.20.1"

### Rule: Must specific
- Wrong: "I can work on this"
- Right: "I can fix the UI of this button and a make it more opacity"

### Rule: Must show my device version
- Wrong: "It worked on my local"
- Right: "It worked on macOS 26.3"

## Things I never post
- Never promised things I can't committed such as unrealistic deadline: today, tomorrow, within this weekends, etc.
